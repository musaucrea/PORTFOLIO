UI/UX Designer · Practice Assessment

Send to M-Pesa: Flow Redesign

Time: 90 minutes Tools: Figma + paper Platform: Android, 360×800

The brief

Co-op Bank's data shows that 38% of customers who start a "Send to M-Pesa" transfer in the app never finish it. Support also gets many complaints:

"I didn't know there was a fee until after I sent."

"I sent money to the wrong number and couldn't reverse it."

"The app froze after I entered my PIN. I didn't know if the money went or not, so I sent again and was charged twice."

Your task: Redesign the Send to M-Pesa flow in the Co-op mobile app to reduce drop-off and errors.

Users

Mama Wanjiru, 52

Murang'a · Dairy farmer · Sacco member
  • Low-end Android phone, patchy 3G
  • Sends money to her children at university
  • Prefers Swahili and is nervous about making mistakes

Brian, 27

Nairobi · Works in marketing
  • Uses the app every day
  • Sends money to friends and his landlord's M-Pesa
  • Wants it fast and hates extra steps

Requirements

Your flow must include:

  • Choosing a recipient: a new number, a phone contact, or a recent or saved recipient
  • Entering the amount and choosing which account to send from
  • Showing the fee and total before the customer confirms
  • Showing the recipient's registered M-Pesa name before sending
  • PIN or fingerprint confirmation
  • A success screen with a receipt that can be shared
  • Three failure states: insufficient funds · wrong or unregistered number · network timeout after the PIN, where the money's status is unknown

Constraints

  • Mobile only, 360×800 frame (Android)
  • Use Co-op green for primary actions. You don't need the real logo.
  • Meet WCAG AA contrast, with tap targets of at least 44 px
  • Transfer limit is KES 150,000 per transaction

What to deliver

  1. A user flow diagram showing all steps and branches
  2. Low-fi sketches of the key screens (photo or quick frames)
  3. 5–8 high-fi screens, including at least 2 of the failure states
  4. Short notes on the canvas explaining your key decisions
  5. A 5-minute presentation: the problem, your solution, edge cases, and how you'd measure success

Stretch goals (only if you have time left)

  • A Swahili version of the confirmation screen
  • A "Save this recipient" prompt after a successful send
  • How the same flow would work on USSD (*667#)

How you'll be assessed

  • Problem understanding: did you tackle the drop-off and the three complaints directly?
  • Flow logic: is the path clear, with every branch accounted for?
  • Edge cases: are the failure states honest, calm and actionable?
  • Visual consistency: are spacing, type and components applied consistently?
  • Accessibility: contrast, tap targets, and labels that don't rely on colour alone
  • Communication: can you explain why you made each decision?
